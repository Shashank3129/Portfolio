POV

Point of view

How I think about product

Strategy

Short-term wins are a tax on long-term products

Every quick feature you ship to close one deal is a loan against your roadmap. You pay it back in maintenance, support tickets, and a muddier product story. The best calls I've made as a PM were the requests I said no to, with data and an alternative in hand.

AI and Product

AI turned PMs back into builders

With AI tooling, a PM can prototype the real product instead of a Figma of it. That's how I shipped three products on my own, and it changed how I scope MVPs, write requirements, and talk to engineers.
